A short list of recent changes that you will notice in daily work. Each item links to the topic that explains it.

## Who uses this

Everyone who signs in to EcoSystem. Some items apply only if you have the related menu.

## 4 November 2026

- **My Compliance.** Every employee can read the compliance rules and training, search them, and report a concern online or on paper, in their own name or anonymously. See [[my-compliance]].
- **Compliance Management.** The Compliance team follows up the reports employees send, exports a recap by day, month, year or date range, and manages the content and the dropdown choices of My Compliance. See [[compliance-management]].
- **Web Report.** Anyone can report an issue from the `Report` button on the sign-in page, and the people who handle reports answer in `Web Report Management`. See [[web-report-management]].
- **Legal Basis.** The Legal `Dashboard` tab is removed. `Legal Basis` now has the tabs `Register` and `Settings`. See [[legal-basis]].

## 3 November 2026

- **Sales Compliance.** Each sales deal can now be sent for a compliance review, with the price against the MRP, the documents and the anti-bribery declarations. Approvers decide from [[my-approvals]]. See [[sales-compliance]].
- **Vendor Onboarding.** Vendor registrations are reviewed step by step, and a verified vendor becomes a Business Partner of type Vendor with one click. See [[vendor-onboarding]].

## 27 October 2026

- **User Guide.** This guide is now part of the application, in English and Indonesian, with search and a PDF download. It shows only the topics for menus you can use. See [[welcome]].
- **Month fields work in Firefox.** Fields that ask for a month and year now show a month picker in Firefox too, so you can choose a period without typing it.
- **Emergency contact is optional for consultants.** When you add or edit a consultant, the emergency contact is no longer required. See [[external-consultants]].
- **Signer warning for External contracts.** If the people who sign External contracts have not been set up, the contract page tells you so before you go further. See [[external-contracts]].
- **Work placement for External contracts.** The `Work placement` list is built from the company branches, and you can add more places in the contract settings. See [[external-contract-settings]].

## 26 October 2026

- **Time picker for attendance corrections.** Times are now chosen with an `HH:MM` picker instead of free typing. See [[my-attendance]].
- **Request correction on My Attendance.** After you check in and check out, a `Request correction` button lets you send a fix for a wrong or missing time. HR reviews it, and you can follow it under `My Correction Requests`. See [[my-attendance]].
- **Paystub fills in automatically.** When Finance has switched on the automatic option in Payroll `Settings`, calculating a period also generates and publishes the slips, so employees see them in `Paystub` straight away. If the option is off, slips appear after Finance clicks `Publish Payslip`. See [[my-paystub]] and [[payroll]].
- **Assignment on External contracts.** An External contract now has an `Assignment` field and uses the signer that has been set for it. See [[external-contracts]].
- **Search box in dropdowns.** In lists with a search box, the box stays in place while you scroll through the options.

## 22 October 2026

- **Faster employee search.** Searching in `Employee` and when you start an Offboarding is quicker and shows results as you type. See [[employee-data]].

## 19 October 2026

- **Timesheet & Payment for consultants.** Consultants have `My Timesheet & Invoice`, and Finance has `Timesheet & Payment` to review timesheets, invoices and payments. See [[external-timesheet-payment]] and [[my-timesheet-invoice]].
- **Offboarding.** The exit process has its own menu. See [[offboarding]].
