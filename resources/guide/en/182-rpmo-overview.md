`RPMO` › `Overview` is the entry page of the Resource and Project Management Office menu. At the moment it holds no tools of its own.

## Who uses this

Roles given the `RPMO` › `Overview` menu in Menu Access, mostly the RPMO team.

## Before you start

- The page states that the RPMO module is under construction. Nothing on it can be changed.

## Step by step

1. Open `RPMO` › `Overview`. You see the title and a note that the features are reached through the sub-menus.
2. Use the sub-menus under `RPMO`. Today the working one is `Period Management`, where timesheet periods are opened, closed and extended.
3. Related views for resources and projects are in `Reporting`, for example `Resource Timeline`, `Weekly Consolidation` and `Consultant Assignment`.

## What happens next

Nothing is changed by opening this page. New RPMO tools will appear as sub-menus when they are released.

## Common questions

**Is something broken, the page is almost empty?** No. It is a placeholder until the RPMO tools are added.
