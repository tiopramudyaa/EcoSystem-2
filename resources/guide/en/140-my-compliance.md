`My Compliance` is the place to learn the company's compliance rules and to report something that breaks them. It holds the knowledge base, the Do & Don't rules, the training schedule and the form to report a concern. You can send a report in your own name or anonymously.

## Who uses this

Every employee. You do not need a special permission. People who look after the content find a `Manage content` button on the page that opens Compliance Management.

## Before you start

- To report a concern, know who or what it is about and roughly when and where it happened.
- Have any proof ready (a document, photo or recording, up to 15 MB). It is optional.
- Decide whether to send it in your name or anonymously. An anonymous report is not linked to you in any way.

## Step by step

### Read the rules and learn

1. Open `My Workspace` › `My Compliance`. The tabs are `Home`, `Knowledge Base`, `Do & Don't`, `Training` and `Report a Concern`.
2. On `Home`, read the `Critical compliance rules`, then browse by category, or open a featured article.
3. In `Knowledge Base`, pick a category on the left to list its articles, then open one to read it.
4. In `Do & Don't`, read what you should do and what you must not do. Each "must not" rule shows how serious it is.
5. In `Training`, see the sessions by month and your progress. Click a session to see the time, the facilitator and the links to join, to watch the recording or to open the material.
6. Use the search box at the top to look for a word across articles, rules, critical rules and training sessions. Type at least 2 characters.

### Report a concern online

1. Open the `Report a Concern` tab.
2. Fill in `Who or what is it about` and describe `What happened`: when, where, what happened and who was involved. Write at least 20 characters.
3. Attach `Supporting evidence` if you have it.
4. Tick `Send anonymously` if you do not want your name stored with the report.
5. Click `Send report`. A window asks `Send this report?` and shows who it is about, who sends it and the evidence file.
6. Click `Yes, send report`, or `Go back and edit` to change something.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A report cannot be changed or withdrawn after it is sent. Check the summary in the window before you confirm.

### Follow a report

1. If you sent it in your name, find it under `My reports` on the same tab. You see its code, its status, the date a follow-up is expected and any message from the administrator.
2. If you sent it anonymously, a tracking code appears once after sending. Write it down. Use it on the public report status page to check the status. It is the only way to follow an anonymous report.

### Report on paper instead

1. On `Home` or `Report a Concern`, click `Download form (PDF)` or `Download bribery & corruption report form`.
2. Fill in the form privately and hand it to the Compliance Committee, or send it by post or to the email address shown.

> [!NOTE]
> Nothing is recorded about who downloads the form.

## What happens next

An administrator reads your report and follows it up. The status moves from `New` through the steps the administrators have set, until it is closed. A report sent in your name shows these changes under `My reports`.

## Common questions

**Who can read my report?** Only the administrators who handle compliance reports. Your name, contact and story are stored encrypted.

**Is an anonymous report really anonymous?** Yes. Nothing links it to your account, so it will not appear under `My reports`. Keep the tracking code.

**Can I add more later?** Not to the same report. Send a new one and mention the earlier code.

**Do I have to use the online form?** No. The printed form is another way to report. It is made for bribery and corruption cases and can be handed in, posted or emailed.
